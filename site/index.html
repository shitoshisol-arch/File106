Can the deployer freeze or seize my tokens?

No. The freeze authority is revoked. The deployer cannot freeze a token account or move tokens from your wallet. View the revocation transaction.

Can the name, logo, or metadata change?

No. The metadata account is locked with isMutable = false. The name, symbol, metadata URI, and logo are permanent. View the lock transaction.
